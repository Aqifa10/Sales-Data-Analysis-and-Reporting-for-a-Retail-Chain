# Sales Data Analysis & Reporting for a Retail Chain

> An end-to-end retail analytics project focused on transforming raw sales data into actionable business insights using Python, SQL, and Excel.

## Overview

Retail businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, but raw sales records alone do not provide a clear view of revenue performance, customer behavior, retention, or sales trends.

This project builds an end-to-end analytics workflow to clean, analyze, segment, and report retail sales data, with the final insights presented through an automated Excel dashboard.

## Problem

The business needed a structured way to:

- Understand sales and revenue performance
- Identify customer purchasing patterns
- Segment customers based on their value and behavior
- Analyze customer retention over time
- Identify sales trends and patterns
- Convert raw transactional data into usable business reports

## Solution

I developed a data analytics workflow combining **Python, SQL, and Excel** to move from raw data to business-ready reporting.

### Analytics Workflow

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → SQL Analysis → Customer Segmentation → Cohort Analysis → Time-Series Analysis → Dashboard & Reporting**

## Key Analysis

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation

- Inspected and prepared the retail dataset for analysis
- Handled data-quality issues and prepared structured data for downstream analysis

### 2. Sales Analysis

- Analyzed sales performance across relevant business dimensions
- Examined revenue trends and changes over time
- Identified patterns that can support business decision-making

### 3. RFM Customer Segmentation

Applied **Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM)** analysis to understand customer purchasing behavior and identify different customer segments based on transaction activity and value.

### 4. Cohort Analysis

Performed cohort analysis to study customer behavior and retention across different customer groups and time periods.

### 5. Time-Series Analysis

Analyzed historical sales data to identify trends and patterns over time.

### 6. Excel Dashboard

Developed an automated Excel dashboard to consolidate key metrics and analytical findings into a business-friendly reporting interface.

## Tech Stack

| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Python** | Data cleaning, preparation & analysis |
